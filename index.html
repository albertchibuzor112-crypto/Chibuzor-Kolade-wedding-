A letter from my heart

To My Husband-to-Be

My sweet love,

Sometimes I think about how God writes the most beautiful stories in the simplest ways.

I thank God for you. I thank God for bringing you my way.

I love you. I love your face. I love your smile. I love your lips. I love your dark skin.

I love how intentional you are. I love how hardworking you are. I love your desire for change and your desire for growth. I love your heart. I love your patience.

I love the little things that make you who you are, and I love that I get to know you in ways the rest of the world may never fully understand.

I am forever grateful to God the Father for you.

As we embark on this journey, I pray that it will end in God. That neither of us will give up, neither of us will grow tired, and neither of us will become weary. May God remain the centre of our home, our love and everything we build together.

I am writing this 39 days to our wedding, and I absolutely cannot wait to be yours.

I cannot wait to do life with you, to grow with you, to pray with you, to laugh with you and to keep choosing you through every season.

I love you from the top of your head to the soles of your feet, and from your inside to your outside.

Thank you for being my answered prayer, my friend and the man I get to call my husband.

Ever and ever before, and ever and ever after.
